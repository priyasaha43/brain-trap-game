Privacy Policy – Brain Trap

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Brain Trap is a browser-based puzzle game. In this version, game progress, coins, scores and preferences are stored locally in your browser using local storage. This information is not sent to a game server by this version.

Sharing

If you choose to use the share feature, your device may open its native share sheet or WhatsApp with a pre-filled challenge message. You decide whether to share it.

Third-party services

This version does not include advertising, analytics, account registration or an online leaderboard.

Children

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online sharing features.

Changes

This policy must be updated before adding accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud saves or a global leaderboard.
